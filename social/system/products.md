## Margin Pro
- **Hero:** "Recover more. Every dollar forensically audited." Sub: specialists "find what other recovery services miss across Amazon, Walmart, Target, and more."
- **Who:** sellers, vendors, brands and resellers, selling to retailers or through marketplaces.
- **Does:** rebuilds every handoff into one ledger, line by line, before filing; finds shortages, short-pays and fee errors; files each claim inside its window and tracks it to payment; appeals and re-files denials ("Denied claims get re-filed, not written off"); builds an Evidence Dossier if a qualifying claim still isn't paid; adds claim types as policies change; read-only access, no risk to the account.
- **Services:** Vendor Recoveries, Seller Reimbursements, Carrier Optimization, 3PL Recoveries (beta), Tariff Refunds (beta).
- **Proof:** $1B+ recovered; 2,000+ brands and resellers; 40+ claim types monitored (home page: 45+ claim scenarios, 24/7 monitoring); 30 days to first recovery for vendor claims, 7 days to first reimbursement for seller claims; 2–5% of marketplace revenue is typically recoverable. Customer results: Celsius $500K+ in eight weeks with under an hour of internal work; Acquco $2M; Grainger $3.5M saved on parcel and LTL; Modway $2.7M a year on shipping; 360 Electrical carrier costs cut 37%.
- **Language:** *recovery* for 1P vendors, *reimbursements* for 3P sellers. The margin frame is leakage: margin leaks across the chain; we find it and get it back.
- **CTAs:** Book a Profit Audit, Get A Free Audit (free, no obligation, 48-hour turnaround).

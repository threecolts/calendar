# Captions

Write captions for every platform the brief names. Default set: Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Facebook. The image carries the hook; the caption adds the one thing the image can't (the how, the number, the next step). Same voice as the brand (see `brand.md`); each platform gets its own version, not a copy.

| Platform | Length | Shape | Hashtags | Links |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | 1–4 short lines; the first ~125 characters show before "more" | Hook line that adds to the image, one line of detail, CTA | 3–5, at the end, specific (#amazonfba #onlinearbitrage), never #success | Not clickable: "Link in bio." |
| LinkedIn | 3–6 short lines, under ~1,000 characters; the first ~200 show before "see more" | Operator-credible: a plain truth, the mechanism, what it means for margin or time, CTA | 0–3 | Allowed in the post |
| X | Under 280 characters, ideally under 200 | One sharp line, optional second line | 0–1 | Allowed; counts as about 23 characters |
| Facebook | 1–3 lines | Friendlier than LinkedIn, plainer than Instagram | 0–2 | Allowed |

Rules:
- Open with the specific thing, not the brand: "Stock you can't sell is the most expensive stock you'll ever buy." not "At Threecolts, we…".
- Name the product once, exactly as written in `brand.md`.
- No emoji as decoration. One is fine where it does a job (a pointer on Instagram).
- No banned words, no invented numbers, no superlatives without a source.
- Carousel captions say what's inside ("Three checks before you source, in four cards.").
- Story frames get no caption; give one short line of sticker text if a link or poll sticker would help.
- Give each caption its character count in brackets so Angela can see it fits.

Output shape: `templates/captions-template.md`.

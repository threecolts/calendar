# Brand summary (from the Threecolts Brand System)

The org's **Threecolts Brand System** artifact is the source of truth. This is the part that matters for social.

## Company and products

Threecolts is the operational infrastructure for marketplace commerce. The company line is "Turn ecommerce complexity into profitable growth." Every piece argues one job the customer hires us for (margin, growth, speed or accuracy) and names the product second.

Products, named exactly: **Seller 365** (subscription with the tools and AI Operator inside), **AI Operator** (then "Operator"; never "the AI"), **Margin Pro**, **UniCon** (never CedCommerce), **Retail 365**, **InventoryLab**, **InventoryLab Accounting**, **Tactical Arbitrage**, **SmartRepricer**, **ScoutIQ**, **ScoutX**, **Scoutify**, **FeedbackWhiz** (Alerts, Emails, Profits).

## Claims

- AI Operator works only with a Seller 365 subscription.
- The tools are only offered within Seller 365, with no standalone prices.
- AI Operator can't produce FNSKU labels. Never show or imply that it can.
- Never write a price, plan, trial length, customer number or result unless the brief gives it. "From $69/month" and "14-day free trial" have appeared before but are unconfirmed (TO-CONFIRM).
- No real competitor names. Soften superlatives ("one of the longest-running").

## Voice

Plain commercial truths about how marketplaces actually work, from a smart colleague who respects your time. Decisive (never "can help"), operator-credible (name the platform, the system, the number), restrained (say less, make it specific).

- Headlines: sentence case, end with a full stop. CTAs: Title Case, no full stop ("Start Free Trial"), and only in captions, never as a button on the image.
- Don't give documents feelings, don't assign intent, don't say "only we". State the mechanism.
- **Banned:** operating system, tool, helper, unlock, empower, game-changer, landscape, headaches, seamless, best-in-class.
- Vocabulary: *recovery* for 1P vendors, *reimbursements* for 3P sellers. Margin frame: margin leaks across the chain; we find it and get it back.

## Type

Inter for everything (in `fonts/`). Weights 400 and 600; 700 only on big stat figures. Headlines track at −0.03em (mixed-weight headlines −0.02em). IBM Plex Mono, uppercase, only for data labels under a stat.

## Colour

- Light canvas `#f4f4f4` with the light wash; dark canvas graphite `#1d1d1d` with the dark wash. Never pure black.
- Text on light: `#1d1d1d` headlines, `#474747` body, `#707070` labels. On dark: white, `#d7d7d7`, `#9a9a9a`.
- On photos: white text.
- Product accents (e.g. Seller 365 `#00a35c`) are for chips and small highlights, never headlines. AI Operator has no flat accent: its identity is the gradient on graphite, so its type cards are `"dark": true`.
- No coral or red for negative moments. No green or orange emphasis words.

## Logos

Not on posts by default (see `look.md`). When a brief asks for one, `"logo": true` puts the product lockup (or the Threecolts lockup on company posts) top left, using the official files in `brand/`. Files for other products: read them from the Threecolts Brand System artifact (Product marks group). Never redraw, recolour or stretch a logo.
